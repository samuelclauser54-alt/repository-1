100 AIs vs an asteroid field. ☄️

Every flyer moves forward on its own; the only control is the engine (hold to climb, let go to fall). A rock about every 4.5 units, each drifting slowly up and down. Touch a rock or a wall and you're out.
• Generation 1: 0 of 100
• Generation 19: 17 of 100
• Generation 63: 36 of 100
• Generation 129: 47 of 100

Each AI is a tiny neural network (18 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: engine power). It feels the walls right where it is and 1.5, 3 and 4.5 units ahead, its vertical speed, and for the 3 nearest rocks ahead: how far away, how big, and where the bigger gap beside it is. Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random fields and get one score: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, in a field they never trained in.

This one is hard. After 129 generations only 47 of 100 make it all the way; most of the rest die in the last few seconds.
