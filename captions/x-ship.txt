100 AIs learning to land a rocket on a ship in a storm. The deck slides side to side AND rises and falls with the waves. 🌊🚀

This is the storm version of the moving-pad reel. The deck is 4.8 units wide, swings 3 units left and right, and heaves 1.3 units up and down on a different rhythm. A landing only counts if it is soft compared with the moving deck, upright, and on the ship. Miss and it's the sea.
• Generation 1: 0 of 100 land
• Generation 24: 22
• Generation 40: 59
• Generation 90: 91

Each AI is a tiny neural network (11 inputs, 12 neurons, 2 outputs: engine and steering). It sees where the ship is, how fast the deck is moving, and its own position, speed and tilt. Every generation, 300 networks fly 4 test flights in random sea states; the top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best.

Nobody told them to match the deck's speed before touching down. They found it on their own, because landing on a rising deck too fast counts as a crash.
