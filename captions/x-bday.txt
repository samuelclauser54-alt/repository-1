How many people need to be in a room before two of them probably share a birthday? Not 183. Just 23. 🎂

I ran it: people walk into a room one at a time and land on their birthday. In this room, person 23 matched person 14.

Then I simulated 10,000 rooms. In half of them, the first shared birthday showed up by person 23.
• 23 people → 50.7%
• 30 people → 70.6%
• 50 people → 97%
• 70 people → 99.9%

Why it feels wrong: you picture YOUR birthday matching someone's, and that really is rare. But 23 people make 253 different pairs, and any one of them can match. 253 chances at a 1-in-365 shot add up fast.
