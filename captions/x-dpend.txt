500 double pendulums. Each one starts a billionth of a degree away from the next.

For 11 seconds they move as one. Then the gap, which doubles every 0.46 s, finally gets big enough to see, and they scatter.

There's no randomness anywhere in this. I simulated every pendulum with the exact same equations of motion (RK4, 1 m arms, g = 9.81). A double pendulum is chaotic: tiny differences grow exponentially. It's the same reason weather forecasts give out after about two weeks.

Sound: each pendulum plays a note set by its height. Together they play one melody, and then 500.
