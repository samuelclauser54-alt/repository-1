Cave series part 3: now there are spikes. 🦇

Every flyer moves forward on its own; the only control is the engine (hold to climb, let go to fall). A spike sticks out of the roof or the floor about every 8 units and reaches halfway across the cave. Touch a spike or a wall and you're out.
• Generation 1: 0 of 100
• Generation 25: 16 of 100
• Generation 69: 67 of 100
• Generation 80: 72 of 100

Each AI is a tiny neural network (9 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: engine power). It only feels how far the roof and floor are (spikes included) right where it is and 1.5, 3 and 4.5 units ahead, plus its own vertical speed. Nobody told it what a spike is. Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random caves and get one score: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, in a cave they never trained in.

Spikes were far harder than a narrow cave: generation 25 still only had 16 of 100 through, and the best it reached in 80 generations was 72.
