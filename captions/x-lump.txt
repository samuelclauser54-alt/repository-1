You get $60,000. Do you invest it all today, or put in $1,000 a month for 5 years so a crash can't catch you? I tested both on 10,000 simulated markets. 📈

• all in on day one: typical balance after 10 years is $104,353
• $1,000 a month: typical balance after 10 years is $92,356
• all in came out ahead in 74% of the markets

The catch: all in is a rougher ride. In the first 5 years it dropped below $50,000 at some point in 31% of the markets. The monthly plan did that in only 11%.

So spreading it out is not the better bet on average. It costs about $12,000 in the typical case, and what you buy with that is a smaller chance of a painful early drop.

How I ran it: monthly returns that add up to 7% a year after inflation with swings of ±17% a year. Cash that is still waiting earns 0% after inflation. The thin lines are 150 of the markets for each plan, and the thick lines are the median.
