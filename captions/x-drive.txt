100 AIs learning to drive. They start out knowing nothing. 🏎️

Each AI is a tiny neural network (5 inputs, 6 neurons, 1 output: how hard to steer). All it sees is 5 distances to the edge of the track, like 5 laser beams fanned out in front of the car. The speed is fixed, so it can't slow down for corners.
• Generation 1: every car is off the track within 1.5 seconds
• Generation 3: 11 of 100 finish a lap
• Generation 5: 73 finish a lap
• Generation 20: all 100 drive for 14 seconds (about 3 laps) without touching the edge

How it learns: every generation, 300 networks drive 3 random tracks and get one number back: how far round they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. No examples, no instructions. The 100 you see are that generation's best, on a track that was never used in training.

It's not a steady climb: in generation 25, 4 of the 100 crashed on this same track before finishing a lap.

This is neuroevolution, the same method as the gates and rocket videos.
