100 AIs learning to parachute onto an island, starting from nothing. 🪂

The wind blows sideways and changes with height: orange streaks blow right, blue blow left, and longer means stronger. Each jumper can steer and can brake its fall. A landing only counts if it is on the island and slow enough. Everything else is a splash.
• Generation 1: 4 of 100 land on the island
• Generation 5: 29
• Generation 8: 83
• Generation 40: 100

Each AI is a tiny neural network (5 inputs, 8 neurons, 2 outputs: steer and brake). It sees where it is, how fast it is drifting, the wind at its height and the wind 2 units below. Every generation, 300 networks make 4 jumps from random places in random wind and get one score: how close they came, plus a bonus for a soft landing on the island. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, all in the same wind.

Nobody told them how wind works. The only feedback was the score.
