Cave series part 7: they can't see what's coming. 🦇

In every earlier part the flyers could feel the walls up to 4.5 units ahead. Now they only feel the roof and floor right where they are and half a unit ahead (about one body length). The only control is the engine.
• Generation 1: 0 of 100
• Generation 11: 17 of 100
• Generation 20: 66 of 100
• Generation 32: 100 of 100

Each AI is a tiny neural network (5 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: engine power). No look-ahead: it only feels the roof and floor where it is and half a unit ahead, plus its own vertical speed. Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random caves and get one score: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, in a cave they never trained in.

I expected this to fail. It took 32 generations instead of 4, but 100 of 100 got through.
