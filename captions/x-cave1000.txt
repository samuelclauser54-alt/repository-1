1,000 AIs learning to fly through a cave. 🦇

Every flyer moves forward on its own. The only control is the engine: hold it to climb, let go to fall. Touch the roof or the floor and you're out. The cave gets narrower the deeper they go.
• Generation 1: 5 of 1,000 made it
• Generation 3: 256 of 1,000 made it
• Generation 5: 791 of 1,000 made it
• Generation 40: 958 of 1,000 made it

Each AI is a tiny neural network (9 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: engine power). It sees how far the roof and floor are right where it is and 1.5, 3 and 4.5 units ahead, plus its own vertical speed. Every generation, 1,000 networks fly 3 random caves and get one score: how far they got. The top 100 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 1,000 you see are that generation's best, in a cave they never trained in.

Ten times more flyers than the original cave video. Generation 1 lost almost all 1,000 in the first few seconds.
