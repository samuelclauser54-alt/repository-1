$5,000 on a credit card. What happens if you only ever pay the minimum? 💳

Two people, same $5,000 balance, 24% APR, no new purchases. Both pay $150 the first month.
• Red pays the minimum every month. As the balance drops, so does the minimum: $150, then $83 after 5 years, then $45 after 10.
• Green keeps paying that first $150 and never lets it shrink.

Result:
• Red: 16 years 9 months, $8,442 in interest
• Green: 4 years 8 months, $3,322 in interest

The minimum is built to shrink with your balance, so most of each payment keeps going to interest. Refusing to let it shrink saves 12 years and about $5,100.

Assumptions: minimum = 1% of the balance plus that month's interest, never under $35, which is a common formula. Your card's terms may differ, so check your statement.
