Part 6: now there's wind. 100 AIs learning to fly through gates while gusts shove them up and down. 🕹️

The wind swings between pushing up (orange) and pushing down (blue) about every 1.2 seconds, and at full strength it is about two thirds as strong as gravity. A flap that was right a moment ago now overshoots.
• Generation 1: 10 of 100 get through the first gate
• Generation 10: 37 get through the first gate, 1 clears all 14
• Generation 25: 41 clear all 14
• Generation 100: 90 clear all 14

Each AI is a tiny neural network (6 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: flap or don't). Two of the inputs tell it what the wind is doing right now. Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random courses and get one number back: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, on a course they were not trained on.
