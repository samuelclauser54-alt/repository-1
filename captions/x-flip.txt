Part 3: gravity flips every 2 seconds. 100 AIs learning to fly through gates anyway. 🕹️

Every 2 seconds, down becomes up. A flap always pushes against gravity, so the same button that saved you a moment ago now throws you into the wall.
• Generation 1: 10 of 100 get through the first gate
• Generation 10: 97 get through the first gate, and not one gets through a second. The first flip kills all of them
• Generation 45: 3 clear all 14 gates
• Generation 75: 75 clear all 14 gates

For about 40 generations they were stuck on the flip. Then a few networks found a way to use the two inputs that tell them which way gravity points and how long until it changes, and that spread through the population.

Each AI is a tiny neural network (6 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: flap or don't). Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random courses and get one number back: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, on a course they were not trained on.
