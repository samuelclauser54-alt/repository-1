Stormy ocean part 2: bigger waves, moving faster. 🌊

Waves now reach 6.4 units (part 1: 5.2) and travel 40% faster, under the same low storm ceiling. Touch the water or the clouds and you're out.
• Generation 1: 0 of 100
• Generation 21: 19 of 100
• Generation 51: 66 of 100
• Generation 97: 75 of 100

Each AI is a tiny neural network (9 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: engine power). It feels how far the clouds and the water are right where it is and 1.5, 3 and 4.5 units ahead, plus its own vertical speed. Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random oceans and get one score: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, in a ocean they never trained in.

Part 1 was solved by generation 28. This one took 51 generations to reach 66, and the best was 75 of 100.
