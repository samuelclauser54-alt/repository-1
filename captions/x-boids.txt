1,500 birds and no leader. Each bird only looks at the birds right next to it. 🐦

Each bird follows 3 rules:
1. don't crash into your neighbors
2. fly the same direction they're flying
3. drift toward the middle of your neighbors

Nobody sees the whole flock and nobody is in charge. They start out scattered in random directions. Within seconds they form flocks, and by the end 93% are flying the same way. Halfway through a hawk shows up: birds near it scatter, and the flock splits around it and re-forms. That's the same move you see in real starling murmurations.

Colors show which direction each bird is heading. The model is "boids," written by Craig Reynolds in 1987 and used in movie animation (Batman Returns used it for bats). I simulated it in NumPy.
