Does the better team actually win the title? I simulated 10,000 playoff series. 🏆

Team A is clearly better: it wins 55% of its games against Team B. They play a best-of-7.
• The better team won the series 60% of the time
• The weaker team took the title 40% of the time

Seven games isn't enough to separate two good teams. Luck doesn't cancel out that fast.

So how long would a series need to be for the better team to win 95% of the time? A best-of-269.

Every playoff format is a trade: short series are exciting because the weaker team has a real shot. A format that reliably crowned the best team would take most of a year.

The 60% comes from 10,000 simulated series; the exact math gives 60.8%. The 55% edge is an assumption. Real match-ups vary, and things like home advantage are left out.
