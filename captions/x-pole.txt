100 AIs learning to balance a stick. Nobody tells them how. 🧠

Each AI is a tiny neural network (4 inputs, 5 neurons, 1 output: push the cart left or right). It starts with the stick already tilted, and gets a random shove every 1.5 seconds.
• Generation 1: every stick is down within seconds. The typical one lasts 0.56 s.
• Generation 5: 4 of 100 last the full 12 seconds
• Generation 8: 27
• Generation 30: 97

How it learns: each generation, 300 networks get 3 tries and one number back: how long the stick stayed up. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. No physics lessons, no examples. The 100 you see are that generation's best.

The physics is the classic cart-pole problem from 1983, one of the oldest tests in reinforcement learning.

Want to run it yourself? I put a live version online where you can change the rules and watch them learn: samuelclauser54-alt.github.io/evolve-lab
