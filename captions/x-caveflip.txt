Cave series part 8: gravity flips every 3 seconds. 🦇

Every 3 seconds up becomes down: gravity switches direction and the engine switches with it (it always pushes against gravity). The walls turn teal while gravity is flipped. Touch a wall and you're out.
• Generation 1: 0 of 100
• Generation 12: 20 of 100
• Generation 20: 68 of 100
• Generation 39: 100 of 100

Each AI is a tiny neural network (9 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: engine power). It feels how far the roof and floor are right where it is and 1.5, 3 and 4.5 units ahead, its vertical speed, and which way gravity points right now. Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random caves and get one score: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, in a cave they never trained in.

It took 20 generations to get past the first flip reliably, and 39 to get all 100 through.
