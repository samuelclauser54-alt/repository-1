Generation 1 vs generation 80 in the spike cave. 🦇

Both groups fly at the same time. Red is generation 1 (random networks), blue is generation 80. A spike sticks out of the roof or floor about every 8 units. Touch a spike or a wall and you're out.
• Generation 1: 0 of 100 made it
• Generation 80: 72 of 100 made it

Each AI is a tiny neural network (9 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: engine power). It only feels how far the roof and floor are (spikes included) right where it is and 1.5, 3 and 4.5 units ahead, plus its own vertical speed. Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random caves and get one score: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, in a cave they never trained in.

Spikes are the hardest cave so far: even generation 80 loses about a quarter of its flyers.
