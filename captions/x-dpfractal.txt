300,000 double pendulums, one per pixel.

Each pixel is its own double pendulum. Its x position sets arm 1's starting angle and its y position sets arm 2's, so neighbors start a hair apart. The color shows both angles at each moment.

Where the picture stays smooth, nearby starts stay close and the motion is predictable. Where it dissolves into static, the motion is chaotic: a tiny change in where you start gives a completely different swing. The calm island in the middle is the low-energy region, where the pendulum can't flip over.

All 291,600 are simulated with the same equations (RK4, 1 m arms, g = 9.81). No randomness anywhere.
