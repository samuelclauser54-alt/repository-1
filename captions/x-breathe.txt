Cave series part 4: the walls move. 🦇

The cave keeps closing and opening: its height shrinks to 40% and back about every 2 seconds, as a wave that travels along the cave. Every flyer moves forward on its own; the only control is the engine. Touch a wall and you're out.
• Generation 1: 1 of 100
• Generation 5: 21 of 100
• Generation 18: 69 of 100
• Generation 60: 91 of 100

Each AI is a tiny neural network (9 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: engine power). It feels how far the roof and floor are right where it is and 1.5, 3 and 4.5 units ahead, plus its own vertical speed. It does not see time, so it has to read the squeeze from the walls themselves. Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random caves and get one score: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, in a cave they never trained in.

The best generation got 91 of 100 through.
