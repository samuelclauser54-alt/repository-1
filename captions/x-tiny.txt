Part 5: the gaps are 26% smaller. 100 AIs learning to fly through gates, starting from nothing. 🕹️

Same flyers and same speed as Part 1. Every gap is 1.4 units tall, down from 1.9, so there is much less room for a late flap.
• Generation 1: 4 of 100 get through the first gate
• Generation 5: 72 get through the first gate, 8 clear all 14
• Generation 30: 46 clear all 14
• Generation 100: 76 clear all 14

In Part 1, with full-size gaps, 80 of 100 cleared the course by generation 40. Here it takes 100 generations to get to 76, and it never reaches 80.

Each AI is a tiny neural network (6 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: flap or don't). Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random courses and get one number back: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, on a course they were not trained on.
