1,000 AIs fly through a pitch-black cave. The only light is their own. 🔦

Every flyer moves forward on its own. The only control is the engine: hold it to climb, let go to fall. Touch the roof or the floor and you're out. The cave gets narrower the deeper they go, and each flyer lights up the rock around it, so when one crashes its light goes out.
• Generation 1: 5 of 1,000 made it
• Generation 3: 256 of 1,000 made it
• Generation 5: 791 of 1,000 made it
• Generation 40: 958 of 1,000 made it

Each AI is a tiny neural network (9 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: engine power). It sees how far the roof and floor are right where it is and 1.5, 3 and 4.5 units ahead, plus its own vertical speed. They don't need light to fly; the darkness is for you, not them. Every generation, 1,000 networks fly 3 random caves and get one score: how far they got. The top 100 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 1,000 you see are that generation's best, in a cave they never trained in.

Generation 1 lost almost all 1,000 in the first few seconds and the cave went dark.
