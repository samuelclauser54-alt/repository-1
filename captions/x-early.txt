Same $200 a month. One person saves from 18 to 30 and then stops for good. The other starts at 30 and keeps going until 65. Who has more at 65? 📈

• early saver: puts in $28,800, typical balance at 65: $296,291
• late saver: puts in $84,000, typical balance at 65: $270,153

I didn't use a fixed 7% like the usual chart. I simulated 10,000 random stock markets (yearly returns averaging 7% after inflation, with real-world swings of ±17%). The early saver ends up ahead in 54% of them. So about 12 years starting at 18 is worth roughly the same as 35 years starting at 30.

The thin lines are 150 of the simulated markets for each saver, and the thick lines are the typical (median) outcome. Balances are in today's dollars.

The best plan is both: start early AND keep going.
