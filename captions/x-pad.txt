Rocket series, part 4: the landing pad is 3 times smaller. 100 AIs learning to hit it. 🚀

Same rocket, same gravity. The pad is 2.6 units wide, down from 8, and a landing only counts if it is soft, upright and on the pad. Landing softly next to it is a crash.
• Generation 1: 0 of 100 land
• Generation 20: 20
• Generation 30: 70
• Generation 110: 99

Each AI is a tiny neural network (7 inputs, 12 neurons, 2 outputs: engine and steering). Every generation, 300 networks fly 4 test flights and get one score (closer to the pad, slower and more upright is better). The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best.

Surprise: the small pad ended up with a better final result (99) than the big one in part 1 (94). With a big pad, "roughly near the middle" was good enough to survive, so there was less pressure to be precise.
