More features can make a model worse. It's a geometry problem.

Scatter 1,000 random points (computed):
• 2 dimensions: the farthest point is 165× farther than the nearest
• 1,000 dimensions: only 1.12× farther

Each extra dimension adds its own random amount to every distance, so in high dimensions everything ends up about equally far away and "nearest neighbor" stops meaning much.

On the Wine dataset (kNN, macro F1, same split as part 1):
• 13 real features: 0.974
• + 1,000 noise columns: 0.644, and the real features are now just 1.3% of each distance
• keep the 13 columns that best separate the grapes (ANOVA F-test, fit on the training set only): 0.974. 12 of those 13 are the real features

(I tried PCA too, and it doesn't fix this one: after scaling, the noise columns have just as much variance as the real ones.)
