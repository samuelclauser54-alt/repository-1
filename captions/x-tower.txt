The landing pad is on top of a tower now. Miss it and you fall all the way down. 100 AIs learning to land on it. 🚀

Same rocket as the rest of the series. The pad is 2.6 units wide on a tower 6 units tall. Touch down soft and upright on the pad and it counts. Clip the side of the tower or miss, and it's a crash.
• Generation 1: 0 of 100 land
• Generation 20: 21
• Generation 30: 83
• Generation 50: 95

Each AI is a tiny neural network (7 inputs, 12 neurons, 2 outputs: engine and steering). Every generation, 300 networks fly 4 test flights from random starts; the top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. Nobody tells them where the pad is or how to fly. The only feedback is the score.

Want to run it yourself? Live demo: samuelclauser54-alt.github.io/evolve-lab

Where should the pad go next? Best idea in the comments gets built.
