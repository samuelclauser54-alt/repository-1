A $2 lottery ticket every week for 50 years. I simulated 10,000 people doing it. 🎟️

Real Powerball odds and prizes (jackpot counted as $40M cash). Each square is one person: red means behind, green means ahead. Gold rings are the big wins.

After 50 years (2,600 tickets, $5,200 each):
• 9,982 of 10,000 lost money
• the typical player is down $4,744
• 17 people hit $50,000 and 1 hit $1,000,000. Nobody hit the jackpot.
• on average each $2 ticket pays back about 46¢

The same $2 a week in an account earning 7% a year grows to about $43,771 after 50 years.

The odds of the jackpot are 1 in 292,201,338. Buying every week for 50 years puts your chance of ever hitting it at about 1 in 112,000.
