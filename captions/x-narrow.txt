Now you can enter your own. I put the trainer online: train a flyer in your browser, give it a nickname and enter it in the Arena. It gets ranked against everyone else's on a course where the gaps keep shrinking, and the top 100 race each other in a video here with their names on screen.
Train one here: samuelclauser54-alt.github.io/evolve-lab

Each AI is a tiny neural network (6 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: flap or don't). Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random courses and get one number back: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, on a course they were not trained on.
