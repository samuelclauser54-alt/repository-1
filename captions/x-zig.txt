Cave series part 6: a zigzag cave. 🦇

Every 5 units the cave changes direction with a sharp corner. Every flyer moves forward on its own; the only control is the engine. Touch a wall and you're out.
• Generation 1: 0 of 100
• Generation 3: 26 of 100
• Generation 5: 71 of 100
• Generation 39: 95 of 100

Each AI is a tiny neural network (9 inputs, 8 neurons, 1 output: engine power). It feels how far the roof and floor are right where it is and 1.5, 3 and 4.5 units ahead, plus its own vertical speed. Every generation, 300 networks fly 3 random caves and get one score: how far they got. The top 30 survive and are copied with small random changes. The 100 you see are that generation's best, in a cave they never trained in.

Surprise: sharp corners were easier than spikes. 71 of 100 by generation 5, 95 by generation 39.

Parts 1 to 5 are on my page. Part 7: they can't see ahead.
