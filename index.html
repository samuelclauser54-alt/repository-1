A private scheduling tool that publishes our own original short videos (code-drawn math, physics and AI simulations) to our own TikTok account on a set schedule.

How it works

The account holder signs in with TikTok and grants posting permission. The holder reviews the upcoming videos, chooses who can see each one and whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, and confirms. The tool then uploads each video at its scheduled time through TikTok's Content Posting API. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings.

What we post

Original simulations we write and render ourselves: neural networks learning by evolution, physics experiments and probability races. No third-party footage, no copyrighted music.
